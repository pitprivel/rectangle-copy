# Privacy Policy – Rectangle Copy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Full text in 7 languages: https://pitprivel.github.io/rectangle-copy/privacy.html

**Rectangle Copy does not collect, store or send any personal data or browsing data.** It has no analytics, no ads and makes no network requests.

- **What it does:** when you hold the selected key and drag, the extension reads the text of the page inside the rectangle – in your browser, on your computer – and puts it on your clipboard. Nothing leaves your device.
- **Settings** (key, column separator, “Copied” message, list of sites where you turned it off) are kept in the browser’s extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If your browser synchronizes extension data with your account, it does so itself; the author has no access to it.
- **Permissions:** access to websites (to draw the rectangle and read the selected text, processed locally and only while you drag), `clipboardWrite` (copying), `storage` (settings), `activeTab` (turning it off on the current site).
- **Links:** “Buy me a coffee” opens buycoffee.to only when you click it; that service’s own privacy policy applies there.
- **Contact:** rectanglecopy@gmail.com or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/pitprivel/rectangle-copy/issues).
